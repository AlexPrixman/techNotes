# Staff workflows

- As an employee, I sign in, see only my assigned notes, create a note for myself, and record completion.
- As a Manager or Admin, I create notes, assign them to active staff, reassign them, and delete them when no longer needed.
- As a Manager or Admin, I create and update staff accounts and deactivate access when necessary.
- As an Admin, I control Admin privileges; Managers cannot elevate themselves or others to Admin.
- As a staff member, I receive clear validation errors, keep my session across refreshes, and can sign out.
- As a maintainer, I run the app from documented environment settings and verify database/browser behavior automatically.

Deleting a staff account requires reassigning or deleting all its notes, including completed ones. Employees cannot delete notes or manage staff accounts. Password changes revoke existing sessions.
